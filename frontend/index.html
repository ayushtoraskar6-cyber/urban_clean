Smart Tech for Cleaner Cities

UrbanClean bridges the gap between civic responsibility and optimized municipality action.

Our Vision

To eliminate trash accumulation in public spaces using smart mapping, automation, and real-time civic participation.

Our Approach

We combine community tracking logs, driver path optimization algorithms, and local government dashboard control into a unified flow.

Our Impact

Helping reduce fuel emissions for garbage collection vehicles by 20-30% through advanced route optimization and shortening dispatch times.

Step-by-Step Resolution Process

Understanding the complaints handling cycle within the UrbanClean network.

1

Citizen Reports Issue

Citizens notice garbage dumps, upload photos, specify details, and share exact coordinates through GPS or interactive map.

Step 1: Citizen Submission
2

Admin Approves & Dispatches

The system catalogs complaints and overlays them on municipality driver paths. Admins monitor high-priority zones.

Step 2: Admin Queue
3

Driver Receives Route & Navigates

Drivers get fuel-optimized point-to-point directions. They follow the GPS pins to collection coordinates.

Step 3: Optimization
4

Cleanup & Verification

Drivers clean the site, capture validation photos, and mark issues as solved. Citizens receive instant confirmation alerts.

Step 4: Cleanup Log
